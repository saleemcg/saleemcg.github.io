AI workflows

I’m actively exploring and integrating emerging AI technologies into my VFX and CGI workflows, building on production experience in lighting, look development, rendering and compositing.

My current focus is on generative AI and node-based workflows using tools such as ComfyUI, Stable Diffusion and FLUX, exploring how they can complement established production pipelines involving Houdini, Maya and Nuke. I’m particularly interested in practical applications such as upscaling, image enhancement, concept development and the integration of AI-generated elements into CG renders and live-action footage.

My approach is to combine the emerging AI technologies with traditional VFX techniques, maintaining artistic control, visual consistency and production quality. I see AI as a valuable extension of the artist’s toolkit, helping streamline technical processes while keeping the final creative decisions firmly artist driven.
