AI workflows

I’m actively exploring how emerging AI technologies can support traditional VFX and CGI workflows, building on my experience in lighting, look development, rendering and compositing.

I’m currently learning and experimenting with tools like ComfyUI, Stable Diffusion and FLUX, focusing on practical uses such as upscaling, image enhancement, concept development and integrating AI-generated elements into CG renders and live action footage.

My goal is to combine AI with VFX techniques to improve efficiency and creativity while maintaining artistic control, visual consistency and production quality.
