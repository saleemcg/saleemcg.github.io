About

I’m Saleem Akthar, a London-based VFX artist specialising in lighting, look development, texturing and rendering.

I’ve had the opportunity to work on a diverse range of projects across film and television, advertising and 3D animation, using a number of industry-standard tools. I’m passionate about visual effects and thrive where technology, art and culture meet to create something new.

I was successfully endorsed for the UK Global Talent visa in the Exceptional Talent category, in recognition of my professional achievements, international impact, and potential to contribute significantly to the UK’s arts sector.
